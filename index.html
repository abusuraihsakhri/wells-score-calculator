References and limitations

Interpretation thresholds are aligned with NICE NG158 for the two-level Wells rules and the 2026 multi-society PE guideline for three-level PE and Revised Geneva categories. Pretest-probability labels are not individualized event probabilities. A negative D-dimer is only interpretable within an appropriate validated algorithm, assay threshold, and clinical context.
